Originated lines · v1

Our own line where no market exists: the VRS model.

Inputs (official): Valve’s Regional Standings of 2026-10-05, published in Valve’s own GitHub repository. The Points column is each team’s Glicko rank value (report.js prints glickoTeam.rank()).

Formula (Valve’s, not ours): Valve evaluates its model by giving every match an expected win rate from the points difference (fit.js, priorWinExpectation, rating deviation 75, using glicko.js). That procedure reduces exactly to E = 1 / (1 + 10^(−g·(Pa − Pb)/400)) with g = 1/√(1 + 3q²·75²/π²) and q = ln10/400. Our test suite ports Valve’s procedure line by line and checks the closed form to 12 decimals.

Calibration (Valve’s published fit): Valve’s model-fit chart reports observed = 0.7986 × expected + 0.126, so the raw expectation is overconfident. We apply the published slope symmetrically, p = 0.5 + 0.7986·(E − 0.5), so both sides sum to 100%.

Identity: exact team labels or the sourced alias register only. Valve’s standings repeat some labels for older rosters, so a label is used only when it is unique or when the current HLTV lineup shares at least three players with exactly one listed roster, Valve’s own roster-continuity rule (team.js). Otherwise no model line is produced.

Where it is used: only when no market line passes its gates. Beside market-priced rows it is shown for comparison and never changes the pick. Every model row is recomputed by the build validator and by the independent auditor.

When Valve’s monthly file cannot resolve a club (model_vrs_mirror): Valve’s GitHub file is published roughly monthly, so a club that entered the standings after its cut-off has no row there and the two-team line cannot run. For exactly those rows, the same points are read from HLTV’s dated view of Valve’s ranking (valve-ranking/teams, e.g. “Valve global ranking on October 22nd, 2026”), which publishes the identical points value and roster per club. The formula, calibration, roster-continuity rule and eight-hour evidence window are unchanged; a page that says “HLTV points” rather than “Valve points”, an unreadable date header, fewer than 100 rows or a duplicated label without a lineup is a recorded miss and the row keeps its research-only pick with no percentage. The row links the dated page and states that the points came from there rather than from Valve’s file.

Live check against the market: across the 8 open matches that have both a market line and a model line in this snapshot, the mean absolute difference is 6.7 percentage points and the favourite agrees in 6 of 8. Disagreements are flagged on the pick board and on the contrarian screen.

Calculation policy · v1

One fixture list. Independent probabilities.

Discovery: Hotspawn CS2 lobby establishes which fixtures belong here. Its CS2 hydration data, the general lobby and CS2 scheduled fixture API are compared by fixture ID and primary fields. All returned CS2 entries remain visible, including stale “scheduled” records. Schema, ID-set and field mismatches are flagged; missing pages are not silently ignored. Conflicting rows cannot supply picks. Partial discovery retains still-open previous-only entries with unverified membership; a wrong-game or unexpected empty result never erases the last good inventory.

Identity: only exact case/punctuation-normalized team pairs and exact UTC starts are accepted. Polymarket’s own published team abbreviations may map to Hotspawn names; those team records are retained with the evidence. The small verified alias register covers only explicitly registered organization labels (WBT, Privateer, BC.Game, EAC, BetBoom, Vitality, Nemiga, G2, Spirit, FURIA, FUT, Natus Vincere, BakS, LPH, Falcons, ROUNDS, Liquid, Aurora, Insiders, Ninjas in Pyjamas, Betclic Apogee Esports, Nemesis, 6666, Hacha, Aurora Young Blud, flacons, ex-CYBERSHOKE, Zetta, Off Chops, Tropa do KinGui, Envy), with primary links and narrow CS2 scope recorded. BetBoom labels were checked on the organization’s official site. A rename is registered only when a dated primary source states it and Valve’s own three-player roster-continuity rule confirms it against the current lineup — for example Liquipedia’s dated note that THUNDERdOWNUNDER became Off Chops on 11 October 2026, whose HLTV lineup shares four players with that standings row. No fuzzy name matching, guessed team aliases, academy/main-team conflation, or assumed start-time corrections. Duplicate candidates are rejected. Known Hotspawn series format, if supplied, must agree with the external source. Where it is not supplied, it remains blank.

Primary picks → research fallback

  1. Discover CS2 from Polymarket’s sports registry, paginate every active event page, then validate the whole-match moneyline. Closed, live, map-only, handicap, totals, stale and malformed markets cannot supply a pick.
  2. Only unresolved matches enter fallback research: try HLTV first, and for any fixture that list does not contain, HLTV’s own dated views (/matches?selectedDate=YYYY-MM-DD: every unmatched fixture’s UTC day first, then the two neighbouring HLTV days, at most fourteen extra reads a run). HLTV groups its list by its own local day rather than by UTC, so without this a match that starts on a later day can collect no HLTV identity at all and end with no pick; with it the same exact opponent pair and exact UTC start are still required, and a listing at a different start is recorded as evidence instead of being corrected. HLTV is a specialist source, not an official betting operator. Current automation may be denied. Ranks and fan votes are not probabilities; no uncalibrated rank-to-win formula is deployed.
  3. Try Pinnacle’s public esports lines with its fixture feed, period 0, a pending parent match, open two-way moneyline and valid future cutoff. Then check Thunderpick and GG.BET for remaining gaps. Their access restrictions or missing validated adapters are recorded separately. Access controls are not bypassed.
  4. Labelled wide-quote tier. For a fixture that would otherwise have no pick at all, an exchange quote that fails only the 25-point spread ceiling can still be accepted — but only when the market is open, accepting orders, has a fresh observation and update, is genuinely two-sided, and holds at least $100 of liquidity. The real spread is published, the row is flagged low confidence, the strict rejection is retained, and a 60-point ceiling still applies. This is a coverage decision, not a claim of precision. An exactly even midpoint inside this tier is rejected rather than published as a 50/50: the midpoint of a spread that already failed the strict ceiling is not a market view, so the row falls through to the model and research tiers below.
  5. Our originated line. If no market line passes its gates, the VRS model prices the match from Valve’s official standings with Valve’s own formula and calibration, after confirming each roster against the HLTV lineup. The row is labelled as our model line, never as a market price. Where a team competes under a new label, the registered rename lets the model read that team’s own standings row (the previous label’s points, verified by roster overlap) instead of refusing the fixture — Ground Zero vs Off Chops is priced this way, from the row Valve lists as THUNDER dOWNUNDER. Pinnacle is tried before the wide exchange tier because a sharp bookmaker line outranks a wide quote. If neither club resolves in Valve’s monthly file, the same points are read from HLTV’s dated view of Valve’s ranking (model_vrs_mirror) before any research tier runs, subject to the same formula, calibration and roster checks; the row publishes that dated page and says the points came from there.
  6. Research-only tiers, no numbers. A fixture with no usable line and no model line may receive a pick from an exact HLTV fixture identity (same opponent pair and UTC start). When both teams’ world rankings are exposed on that page the better-ranked team becomes the pick (research_rank); when exactly one team carries a published world ranking there and the other appears in the same fixture without one, that team becomes the pick (research_rank_single). Each row stores its per-team evidence — fixture label, HLTV label, rank, HLTV ranking link and the primary link for any label mapping — so the pick is re-derivable from the snapshot alone. The HLTV link is published, the row is labelled research-only, and probabilities and fair odds stay blank. This is a ranking-order signal, never a market probability.
  7. Team pages when HLTV has no fixture identity. If HLTV carries only a TBA bracket slot for the fixture, each club label is resolved to exactly one HLTV team page by that page's own heading; the page's published starting five is then the roster evidence Valve's continuity rule needs, and each page's published world rank (or HLTV's own words that the club has no world rank) may supply the same research-only ranking pick as the fixture page would — stored with the team-page record, never presented as a fixture page. A failed, ambiguous or non-exact match leaves the row unresolved and is recorded as such. The page never supplies a probability.
  8. One-sided Valve standings tier, no numbers. If HLTV never listed the fixture at all, a row can still be resolved when exactly one team resolves in Valve’s official global standings under the model’s own identity rules (unique label, or a current HLTV lineup sharing at least three players with exactly one listed roster) and the other team’s label is genuinely absent from that file (research_vrs_single). A present-but-unresolvable label is not absent, and an HLTV-displayed VRS rank for the supposedly absent team blocks the tier. The pick is the listed team; no probability is derived from a single points value.
  9. Recent-form tier, no numbers — the weakest sourced rung. If every tier above fails but HLTV lists the exact fixture, the completed results HLTV publishes on that match page for both teams are compared over an equal window (each side’s last N matches, N = the smaller recorded history, at least 3 and at most 10). More wins is the pick (research_form); an exact tie falls to HLTV’s lifetime head-to-head series wins for the pairing, and a still-level row stays unresolved — no coin is flipped. Recent form ignores opponent strength and roster changes, so it is labelled research-only, publishes no percentage, and every result used is stored on the row for re-derivation.
  10. If no tier applies, probabilities, fair odds and the pick all stay blank. Unresolved picks gives the reason and every attempted source. There is no claim that a pick can always be generated, and no default 50/50.

Our lines: calculated, not guessed

For decimal odds d₁, d₂, compute qᵢ = 1 / dᵢ, then pᵢ = qᵢ / (q₁ + q₂). The overround is q₁ + q₂ − 1. American odds convert to decimal using 1 + A/100 for positive A, or 1 + 100/|A| for negative A. Our fair decimal line is 1 / pᵢ.

For two exchange outcome prices x₁, x₂, use pᵢ = xᵢ / (x₁ + x₂). This is market-implied analysis, not an independently calibrated statistical forecast or a tradable quote. It excludes trading fees. We keep the primary accepted source; fallback lines do not overwrite primary picks or pretend to be a multi-book consensus.

A directional pick is the team with the larger probability; exactly equal verified prices establish no directional edge and are retained as source context while HLTV, books and independently sourced model/research fallbacks continue. A fallback model line is not the exchange’s price. Unknown matches never default to 50/50. Display percentages are rounded to one decimal and complement to 100; downloadable data keeps full precision.

Quality and freshness gates

  • Evidence must be no more than eight hours old, using both observation time (adjusted for HTTP cache Age) and exchange market update time. One expiry deadline also includes fixture age, match start, Hotspawn lock and bookmaker cutoff, and is enforced in the build and browser. A market update is not proof of a recent trade.
  • Exchange liquidity must be at least $100, with a valid two-sided bid/ask and spread no greater than 25 percentage points. Spreads over 10 points are flagged as low precision. These are conservative project policy thresholds, not validated accuracy guarantees.
  • Bookmaker overround must fall between −2% and 25%. Pinnacle does not supply a price-change timestamp in this response; that limitation is shown. One bookmaker is not consensus.
  • Lock times come from Hotspawn’s feed. Recommendations are withheld at lock, not only at match start. Not-open records are not claimed to be completed results.
  • Schedule: four publication attempts per hour at :04, :19, :34 and :49 UTC via GitHub Actions. A separate watchdog checks the live build receipt every 10 minutes and dispatches the same verified workflow only when the published snapshot is older than 90 minutes, with a 45-minute cooldown, a ten-dispatch daily budget and a hard stop if the receipt cannot be read. Historical measurements on 16 October 2026 found delayed and missed runs under the earlier, slower schedule; the detailed diagnosis is linked in the repository. GitHub may still delay or drop jobs or disable schedules; no exact delivery guarantee is possible. The last successful snapshot is retained on failure, with a prominent stale warning, and never relabeled fresh.
  • Bookmaker reachability is measured, not assumed: the last research probe read twelve endpoints from a normal internet egress. Polymarket's Gamma API and Pinnacle's guest esports API returned usable JSON; GG.BET (page and public sport API), Thunderpick's CS2 page, Oddschecker and Pinnacle's outright endpoint refused access (403/401), and api.thunderpick.com does not resolve. So for a low-tier fixture the honest ceiling is 'no accessible two-sided line', which is why such rows fall to our model line or a labelled research pick instead of an invented number.

Verification scope

Every displayed match fact is traced to the official Hotspawn response; this verifies what the source reported, not that contradictory source records are correct. Accepted inputs retain their publisher’s captured fields, source receipts and observation time. Direct operator responses are distinguished from third-party listings and ranking mirrors; a bookmaker link in a listing is not independent confirmation of an operator price tick. Calculated probabilities are our derived estimates, not official forecasts. We checked the initial snapshot row by row with executable assertions and source links; future data is verified by the same schema, identity, market-type, freshness and arithmetic rules. This does not certify team rosters, the true chance of winning, bookmaker accuracy, or every website on the internet.
