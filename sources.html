Calculation policy · v1

One fixture list. Independent probabilities.

Discovery: Hotspawn CS2 lobby establishes which fixtures belong here. Its CS2 hydration data, the general lobby and CS2 scheduled fixture API are compared by fixture ID and primary fields. All returned CS2 entries remain visible, including stale “scheduled” records. Schema, ID-set and field mismatches are flagged; missing pages are not silently ignored. Conflicting rows cannot supply picks. Partial discovery retains still-open previous-only entries with unverified membership; a wrong-game or unexpected empty result never erases the last good inventory.

Identity: only exact case/punctuation-normalized team pairs and exact UTC starts are accepted. Polymarket’s own published team abbreviations may map to Hotspawn names; those team records are retained with the evidence. The small verified alias register covers only explicitly registered organization labels (BetBoom, Falcons and ROUNDS), with primary links and narrow CS2 scope recorded. BetBoom labels were checked on the organization’s official site. No fuzzy name matching, guessed team aliases, academy/main-team conflation, or assumed start-time corrections. Duplicate candidates are rejected. Known Hotspawn series format, if supplied, must agree with the external source. Where it is not supplied, it remains blank.

Primary picks → research fallback

  1. Discover CS2 from Polymarket’s sports registry, paginate every active event page, then validate the whole-match moneyline. Closed, live, map-only, handicap, totals, stale and malformed markets cannot supply a pick.
  2. Only unresolved matches enter fallback research: try HLTV first. HLTV is a specialist source, not an official betting operator. Current automation may be denied. Ranks and fan votes are not probabilities; no uncalibrated rank-to-win formula is deployed.
  3. Try Pinnacle’s public esports lines with its fixture feed, period 0, a pending parent match, open two-way moneyline and valid future cutoff. Then check Thunderpick and GG.BET for remaining gaps. Their access restrictions or missing validated adapters are recorded separately. Access controls are not bypassed.
  4. If none establishes a verified price, probabilities and fair odds stay blank. Unresolved picks gives the reason and every attempted source. There is no claim that a pick can always be generated.

Our lines: calculated, not guessed

For decimal odds d₁, d₂, compute qᵢ = 1 / dᵢ, then pᵢ = qᵢ / (q₁ + q₂). The overround is q₁ + q₂ − 1. American odds convert to decimal using 1 + A/100 for positive A, or 1 + 100/|A| for negative A. Our fair decimal line is 1 / pᵢ.

For two exchange outcome prices x₁, x₂, use pᵢ = xᵢ / (x₁ + x₂). This is market-implied analysis, not an independently calibrated statistical forecast or a tradable quote. It excludes trading fees. We keep the primary accepted source; fallback lines do not overwrite primary picks or pretend to be a multi-book consensus.

A directional pick is the team with the larger probability; exactly equal verified prices get “no directional edge.” Unknown matches never default to 50/50. Display percentages are rounded to one decimal and complement to 100; downloadable data keeps full precision.

Quality and freshness gates

  • Evidence must be no more than eight hours old, using both observation time (adjusted for HTTP cache Age) and exchange market update time. One expiry deadline also includes fixture age, match start, Hotspawn lock and bookmaker cutoff, and is enforced in the build and browser. A market update is not proof of a recent trade.
  • Exchange liquidity must be at least $100, with a valid two-sided bid/ask and spread no greater than 25 percentage points. Spreads over 10 points are flagged as low precision. These are conservative project policy thresholds, not validated accuracy guarantees.
  • Bookmaker overround must fall between −2% and 25%. Pinnacle does not supply a price-change timestamp in this response; that limitation is shown. One bookmaker is not consensus.
  • Lock times come from Hotspawn’s feed. Recommendations are withheld at lock, not only at match start. Not-open records are not claimed to be completed results.
  • Schedule: every six hours at :17 UTC via GitHub Actions. GitHub may delay jobs or disable inactive public-repository schedules. No four-to-eight-hour delivery guarantee is possible. The last successful snapshot is retained on failure, with a prominent stale warning, and never relabeled fresh.

Verification scope

Every displayed match fact is traced to the official Hotspawn response; this verifies what the source reported, not that contradictory source records are correct. Every accepted price retains the official operator’s raw fields and timestamp. Calculated probabilities are explicitly derived. We checked the initial snapshot row by row with executable assertions and source links; future data is verified by the same schema, identity, market-type, freshness and arithmetic rules. This does not certify team rosters, the true chance of winning, bookmaker accuracy, or every website on the internet.
