Evidence ladder & source rules

What is tried, in order, before a blank is published

This is the publication ladder from the method page. Hotspawn supplies the fixture list only; its crowd percentages are never used as a probability.

  1. 1. Polymarket exchange line, strict gate

    Two-sided, funded and fresh: spread 25 points or less, at least $100 of liquidity, and an observation no older than eight hours. De-vigged from both outcome prices.

    Source: Polymarket Gamma API ↗

  2. 2. HLTV fixture identity, rankings and lineups

    Confirms the exact opponent pair and UTC start, the world ranking and the current five. HLTV’s own betting table exposes no numeric two-way price to this collector — re-checked on 25 Oct 2026 against hltv.org/matches/2399918 and its betting/analytics/2399918 odds overview, where the Betting panel rendered one partner promo row with no price and the overview rendered no numeric price for either side — and its community vote is never used as a probability.

    Source: HLTV match pages ↗

  3. 3. Pinnacle esports moneyline

    A sharp bookmaker’s two-way price for the exact fixture, de-vigged with the same formula. One bookmaker is not a consensus.

    Source: Pinnacle guest esports API ↗

  4. 4. Wide exchange quote, labelled low confidence

    Used only when a row would otherwise have no pick at all: still two-sided, funding and freshness gates unchanged, spread between 25 and 60 points. The real spread is published.

    Source: Polymarket Gamma API ↗

  5. 5. Researched bookmaker listing

    A dated two-way price read from a listing page, accepted only when it re-confirms the exact pair, the exact UTC start and an asymmetric pair bound to both team labels. The identical 1.8 / 1.8 placeholder some listings serve is refused, never published.

    Source: EGamersWorld CS2 listing ↗

  6. 6. Our own VRS model line

    Valve’s official Regional Standings points through Valve’s own published expected-win formula and calibration. Labelled as our model everywhere; never presented as a market price.

    Source: Valve counter-strike_regional_standings ↗

  7. 7. Our VRS model from HLTV’s dated Valve ranking

    Only when Valve’s monthly file cannot resolve the clubs: the same formula and calibration, with points from HLTV’s dated Valve-points list, explicit provenance and the same expiry gates. Not an operator price or a claim that HLTV’s current points occur in the older monthly file.

    Source: HLTV dated Valve ranking ↗

  8. 8. Research pick, no percentage

    Ranking-order evidence for an exact fixture — or, when HLTV lists no fixture page, each club’s world rank on its own exact HLTV team page (an unranked side counts only where HLTV states the absence in words) — one side listed in Valve’s official standings while the other side is absent from that file, or — weakest and last — HLTV’s own recent completed results for both teams on the fixture page (more wins over the same number of recent matches; HLTV’s lifetime head-to-head series wins break a tie; still level stays unresolved). A directional pick only — the percentage and the fair odds stay blank.

    Source: HLTV rankings and Valve standings ↗

Limitations for generating these picks

  • No book lists the match. Low-tier and qualifier fixtures are often absent from Polymarket and Pinnacle, the two accessible line sources; Thunderpick and GG.BET gave this collector no validated automated quote, and access controls are not bypassed. Source status →
  • A market exists but fails a gate. Stale updates, spreads wider than 25 points (60 in the labelled wide tier) or under $100 of liquidity are shown as rejected observations below, never published as a percentage.
  • Names differ between sites. Odds move between sources only for an exact team pair and UTC start, or a label in the verified alias register; a renamed or academy label that no primary source links stays unresolved rather than guessed.
  • HLTV has no usable line. HLTV fixture pages confirm identity, lineups and rankings; their betting tables expose no independently confirmed numeric two-way line to this collector. Re-checked 25 Oct 2026 on Tropa do KinGui vs Yawara: the match page’s Betting panel rendered one partner promo row with no price, and the fixture’s odds & prediction overview rendered no numeric price for either side. HLTV fan votes are never used as a probability.
  • HLTV has a bracket slot but no team names. When HLTV publishes a TBA slot (as it did for benched gods vs TitanFlow on 22 October), the fixture page cannot confirm a roster. The two clubs' own HLTV team pages are then read by exact label and used only to tell repeated Valve standings labels apart; the row is labelled research-only and carries no percentage if one side is missing from Valve's published standings.
  • Our model needs both teams. The VRS model prices a match only when both teams resolve in Valve’s official standings with their current rosters. With one side missing it degrades to a pick-only research tier, and with neither side resolvable the row stays unresolved with the attempts listed below.
  • Timing. Every percentage expires at the earliest of its 8-hour evidence window, the Hotspawn lock, match start or bookmaker cutoff, so a pick can disappear before a match begins if its evidence ages out and no refresh lands.
  • Some books post no line at all. On 21 Oct 2026 the Thunderpick affiliate rendering (bo3.gg) carried no two-way line for any DFRAG Invitational fixture or for benched gods vs TitanFlow while pricing control fixtures in the same events; Kalshi’s regulated CS2 series had zero open markets; GG.BET answered HTTP 403. Absence of a line is published as a limitation, never as 50/50. Flagged: DFRAG no-line · Flagged: source availability
  • Sources can disagree about the fixture itself. HLTV lists benched gods vs TitanFlow at a different instant than Hotspawn, EGamersWorld and bo3.gg, and the 22:00Z slot of CCT South America 2026 Series #7 is Zetta vs LOS NINOS on Hotspawn and HLTV but Alianza Lima Esports vs Los Niños on bo3.gg and Polymarket. How picks are made

Evidence before a favorite.

  1. Find every Hotspawn CS2 fixture

    Compare the public lobby’s structured data with its own fixture feed.

  2. Price it from the market

    Validated exchange prices (Polymarket) or a bookmaker line (Pinnacle), normalized or de-margined.

  3. Originate our own line where no market exists

    Valve’s official standings points through Valve’s published formula and calibration, with HLTV lineups confirming each roster.

  4. Then research, never guess

    HLTV-verified identity and ranking order, or one-sided presence in Valve’s official standings, as a research-only pick with no percentage. Anything left stays unresolved.
