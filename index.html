How picks are made

Evidence before a favorite.

  1. Find every Hotspawn CS2 fixture

    Compare the public lobby’s structured data with its own fixture feed.

  2. Price it from the market

    Validated exchange prices (Polymarket) or a bookmaker line (Pinnacle), normalized or de-margined.

  3. Originate our own line where no market exists

    Valve’s official standings points through Valve’s published formula and calibration, with HLTV lineups confirming each roster.

  4. Then research, never guess

    HLTV-verified identity and ranking order as a research-only pick with no percentage. Anything left stays unresolved.
