How these matches are researched

Every source tried, in order, before a blank

  1. 1. Polymarket exchange line, strict gate

    Two-sided, funded and fresh: spread 25 points or less, at least $100 of liquidity, and an observation no older than eight hours. De-vigged from both outcome prices.

    Source: Polymarket Gamma API ↗

  2. 2. HLTV fixture identity, rankings and lineups

    Confirms the exact opponent pair and UTC start, the world ranking and the current five. HLTV’s own betting table exposes no numeric two-way price to this collector — re-checked on 25 Oct 2026 against hltv.org/matches/2399918 and its betting/analytics/2399918 odds overview, where the Betting panel rendered one partner promo row with no price and the overview rendered no numeric price for either side — and its community vote is never used as a probability.

    Source: HLTV match pages ↗

  3. 3. Pinnacle esports moneyline

    A sharp bookmaker’s two-way price for the exact fixture, de-vigged with the same formula. One bookmaker is not a consensus.

    Source: Pinnacle guest esports API ↗

  4. 4. Wide exchange quote, labelled low confidence

    Used only when a row would otherwise have no pick at all: still two-sided, funding and freshness gates unchanged, spread between 25 and 60 points. The real spread is published.

    Source: Polymarket Gamma API ↗

  5. 5. Researched bookmaker listing

    A dated two-way price read from a listing page, accepted only when it re-confirms the exact pair, the exact UTC start and an asymmetric pair bound to both team labels. The identical 1.8 / 1.8 placeholder some listings serve is refused, never published.

    Source: EGamersWorld CS2 listing ↗

  6. 6. Our own VRS model line

    Valve’s official Regional Standings points through Valve’s own published expected-win formula and calibration. Labelled as our model everywhere; never presented as a market price.

    Source: Valve counter-strike_regional_standings ↗

  7. 7. Our VRS model from HLTV’s dated Valve ranking

    Only when Valve’s monthly file cannot resolve the clubs: the same formula and calibration, with points from HLTV’s dated Valve-points list, explicit provenance and the same expiry gates. Not an operator price or a claim that HLTV’s current points occur in the older monthly file.

    Source: HLTV dated Valve ranking ↗

  8. 8. Research pick, no percentage

    Ranking-order evidence for an exact fixture — or, when HLTV lists no fixture page, each club’s world rank on its own exact HLTV team page (an unranked side counts only where HLTV states the absence in words) — one side listed in Valve’s official standings while the other side is absent from that file, or — weakest and last — HLTV’s own recent completed results for both teams on the fixture page (more wins over the same number of recent matches; HLTV’s lifetime head-to-head series wins break a tie; still level stays unresolved). A directional pick only — the percentage and the fair odds stay blank.

    Source: HLTV rankings and Valve standings ↗
