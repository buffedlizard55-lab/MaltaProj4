Methodology / Hotspawn coverage

Evidence before confidence.

How the calculation works

For positive American odds A, raw implied probability = 100 / (A + 100). For negative odds A, it is |A| / (|A| + 100). With raw probabilities q₁ and q₂, our fair estimate is pᵢ = qᵢ / (q₁ + q₂). The larger p is the pick. Fair decimal odds = 1 / p.

Example (illustrative arithmetic only): −150 and +120 become 56.90% and 43.10% after removing the margin. Prediction-market prices are normalized by their sum and converted to equivalent odds for display. A single source is not consensus. We do not claim a profitable betting edge or calibrated accuracy.

Acceptance rules

  • Exact team identity, same competition, same date and match-winner market; ambiguous aliases are rejected.
  • Never substitute map handicaps, outright tournament odds, fan votes or post-match results.
  • Evidence expires after eight hours for recommendations. Older prices remain labeled historical, not actionable.
  • UTC only where confirmed; lobby-local display times are not silently converted.
  • Failed collectors retain the last good snapshot and publish the error. Partial inventory is always labeled partial.
