What you need to know

Probabilities are our own margin-removed calculations from independently sourced two-sided match-winner odds or prediction-market prices—not Hotspawn voting percentages. They are market estimates, not verified facts about the future.

Unresolved picks: Missing markets, unverified team aliases, uncertain schedules and insufficient historical evidence prevent defensible estimates. Every unresolved row and limitation stays visible; no default 50/50 probabilities are invented.

Update target: GitHub Actions checks sources every six hours. Scheduled runs may be delayed. The latest attempt and last successful inventory observation are shown below; failed fetches never make old data look new.

Research limitations

The lobby’s weekly and filter counts disagree. Some tournament filters refer to historical events. Read the flagged irregularities before assuming complete coverage.
