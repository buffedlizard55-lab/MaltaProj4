## Contrarian screen — what the sourced facts support, and what they do not

Hotspawn awards a fixed number of points for a correct pick; multipliers come from tier, stage
and format, not from how the crowd split (see
[How it works](https://play.hotspawn.com/how-it-works) and
`verification-3c8d1136/LIVE-BOARD-VERIFICATION.md`). A crowd-contrarian pick therefore earns
exactly what a crowd-following pick earns when it is right. Contrarianism is only worth
anything when the favourite is genuinely wrong.

- The day-one upset is not evidence that upsets pay: the same pairing reversed 2-0 two days
  later ([45736](https://www.hltv.org/news/45736/aurora-beat-9z-to-secure-third-consecutive-top-four-appearance)).
- Semi-final 1 is a genuine coin flip (52.5/47.5) — the only day-4 fixture where a
  differentiation pick does not cost much probability.
- Semi-final 2 is not: 79.5/20.5. BETBOOM at 20.5% is a long-shot differentiation, not a value
  claim, and the rest asymmetry argues the same way as the market.
