## Contrarian-but-not-stupid framework used on the analysis page

1. Recent, structural roster change (new IGL + new star + new coach) — verify from official announcements and the
   transfer timeline.
2. Favourite demonstrably fatigued and not yet repriced — verify the recent match load from results pages and compare
   with the offered price.
3. Underdog band of roughly 15–40% — above that the crowd already covers it, below it is a lottery ticket.
4. Never on an unverified identity or an unsourced number — the identity register requires exact opponent and UTC start.
5. Cap the number of contrarian swings per slate.

Explicitly unverified: Hotspawn's pick'em scoring rules were not collected, so no claim is made about the relative value
of a correct underdog. No calibrated historical model exists in this project.
