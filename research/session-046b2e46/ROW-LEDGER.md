## Method limits that a reader must know

- A Polymarket midpoint is a **market** estimate, not a forecast: it is the middle of the live two-sided quote and moves with the order book.
- A wide-quote row (spread above 25 points) is published at the market mid and labelled low-confidence; the true value is less precise than the number suggests.
- A bookmaker listing is a **de-vigged** pair: the book's margin is removed proportionally. Listing pages carry no price-update timestamp, so the observation time is what is shown.
- Valve VRS lines are **our own originated lines**, not market prices: Glicko expectation from Valve's official points ([formula](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings/blob/eec4853d7bcf0f1f7efe7f0d901234bef7e98b75/model/fit.js)) calibrated with Valve's published fit slope ([calibration](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings/blob/eec4853d7bcf0f1f7efe7f0d901234bef7e98b75/modelfit.png)). They are used only where no market line passed its gates.
- Hotspawn supplies **fixture inventory and lock times only**. No probability on this site comes from Hotspawn, from crowd votes, or from an inferred value.
