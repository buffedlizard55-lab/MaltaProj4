HOW IT WORKS

Hotspawn Pick'Ems is your chance to predict, compete and win. Make your picks, earn points and climb the leaderboard for a shot at weekly prizes.

1. Make Your Picks

Browse upcoming matches and pick the team you think will win. Picks lock 5 minutes before the scheduled start time, so make sure you submit before the countdown reaches zero.

2. Earn Points

Earn points for every correct pick. Higher tier matches reward more points. The better you predict, the higher you climb.

3. Build Winning Streaks

Get bonus points for consecutive correct picks. The longer your streak, the bigger the bonus.

4. Climb the Leaderboard

Compete against other players and rise through the ranks. The leaderboard resets every week so everyone has a fresh chance to be #1.

5. Win Weekly Prizes

Top performing players can win exclusive weekly prizes including voucher codes.

6. How Winners Are Contacted

Winners will be contacted via the registered account email with instructions on how to claim their prizes.
