## 1. Method, and what this can and cannot prove

* The authoring sandbox has no route to `www.hltv.org` (`curl` → `SSL_ERROR_SYSCALL`, re-measured 2026-10-17). Every page here was read by `.github/workflows/harvest-79f0e55b.yml` on a runner with normal outbound access, and the **whole body** was committed beside the numbers taken from it. Offline replay (`python -m scripts.hltv_verify_79f0e55b --replay research/hltv-79f0e55b`) reproduces the tables from those bytes, and all 20 replayed hashes equal the network receipts recorded at capture time.
* This verifies **what the sources published**. It does not certify that a team will win, that a bookmaker is correct, or that a market price is fair.
* HLTV renders clock times in the **viewer's** timezone and never names it, so a displayed clock is not a fact. Where a start time matters, this report uses the absolute `data-unix` epoch from HLTV's own markup; the displayed clock is kept beside it only as a rendering.
