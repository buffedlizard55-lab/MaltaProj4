THE RESOLUTION LADDER

Evidence first. Fallbacks second.

  1. Hotspawn → fixture inventory only. Parse public Angular transfer-state JSON from the dedicated CS2 lobby. Keep stable fixture IDs, team names, tiers and UTC timestamps. Compare the embedded count with “All tiers”. Never ingest voting percentages into the model.
  2. Polymarket → external series moneyline. Discover the CS2 series from the official sports metadata API and exhaust event pagination. Accept only active, open, order-accepting, two-team moneylines with positive liquidity, updated within eight hours. Exact names and start times within one hour must match uniquely. No fuzzy academy/main-roster matching.
  3. Only missing market matches → HLTV, then esportsbooks. Try HLTV’s public match page, GG.BET, Thunderpick, Pinnacle and Rivalry. Access and parsing limitations are recorded per match. A successful HTML response alone is not a verified line. No CAPTCHA or regional restriction bypass.
  4. Still unresolved → Valve research model. Use the latest non-future global standings, no older than 35 days. Require unique exact-name matches for both teams. No invented default rating for unranked teams.
  5. Still missing evidence → blank. Publish the match, source trail and reason. Retry every refresh. Locked or ended matches are retained without invented retrospective picks.
HOW WE CALCULATE

Our probabilities & fair lines

Prediction markets: for observed prices q₁ and q₂, p₁ = q₁ / (q₁ + q₂); p₂ = 1 − p₁. This removes the total-price imbalance, not all market biases, fees or spread effects.

Sportsbook formula: if a validated two-sided decimal quote becomes available, p₁ = (1/o₁) / (1/o₁ + 1/o₂). No sportsbook quotes are invented when access fails.

Research model v1: p₁ = 1 / (1 + 10g(R₂−R₁)/400), where R is published Valve points and g = 1 / √(1 + 3(ln(10)/400)² × 350² / π²).

The fixed rating deviation of 350 is our conservative modeling assumption, not an observed team statistic. The equation follows Valve’s public Glicko expected-score function. This is not Valve’s official forecast and has not been independently calibrated. No BO1/BO3 adjustment or roster inference is made.

Our fair line: 1 / p. This is a calculated decimal line, never a claim that a bookmaker offers it. The higher probability is the pick; exactly equal probabilities mean no lean.

Automation, provenance & coverage limits

GitHub Actions is scheduled at 00:17, 06:17, 12:17 and 18:17 UTC. Schedules can be delayed or disabled by GitHub; this is not a real-time service or a guaranteed six-hour SLA. The next nominal refresh after this build is around 14 Oct · 10:47 UTC.

A failed fixture refresh retains the previous fixture inventory with its original observation timestamp, but blanks actionable probabilities. The page warns and suppresses picks after eight hours. Expired lock times also suppress displayed picks in JavaScript-enabled browsers. Without JavaScript, always check the printed observation and lock times.

The public lobby currently includes old “scheduled” rows. These are retained and flagged, not silently treated as new matches. Missing pagination counts, duplicate fixture IDs and changed source schemas fail closed. No claim is made about fixtures hidden behind authentication or omitted by Hotspawn itself.

Market identity requires exact normalized names; different spelling or a rescheduled start over one hour will fall back rather than risk assigning the wrong line. Model input dates, points and listed rosters are retained in each row’s evidence. Current rosters are not confirmed.
